Procurement Intelligence Hub

A centralized reporting solution that lets procurement managers monitor spend, supplier performance, purchase orders, and delivery timelines across 5M+ records.

SQLPower BIPower QueryDAX

Business problem

The procurement team bought from many suppliers across regions and categories, but relied on multiple Excel reports spread across different files. Reporting was manual and slow, and there was no complete view of performance. Questions like "Which supplier has the highest spend?", "Which suppliers deliver late?", and "How is spend changing over time?" took a lot of effort to answer.

Objective

Build one centralized reporting solution that gives procurement managers full visibility into spend, supplier performance, purchase orders, and delivery timelines.

My role and approach

  • Gathered business requirements from stakeholders
  • Prepared and cleaned five procurement datasets
  • Designed a star schema: one fact table and four dimension tables
  • Built KPIs with 20+ DAX measures: Total Procurement Spend, Purchase Orders, Active Suppliers, Average Delivery Days, On-Time Delivery %
  • Developed an interactive dashboard with filters for supplier, product category, region, and time period

Insights and outcomes

  • Identified high-spend suppliers and tracked supplier delivery performance
  • Showed procurement spend trends over time and compared spend across regions and product categories
  • Gives managers evidence to negotiate with suppliers, monitor delivery, and optimize procurement costs
  • Monthly reporting cut from 8 hours to 2 hours

Customer Segmentation for Targeted Marketing

Analysis of 1M+ customer records to find meaningful segments from demographics, purchase history, loyalty, and satisfaction data, so marketing could move from one-size-fits-all campaigns to targeted ones.

PythonSQLExcel

Business problem

The business held a large customer database covering demographics, purchase history, loyalty details, and satisfaction data, yet every customer received similar marketing campaigns despite very different buying behavior. Campaigns were less effective and engagement could not be personalized.

Objective

Analyze customer behavior and identify meaningful customer segments so the business could build targeted marketing strategies.

My role and approach

  • Understood the business objective and explored the customer data
  • Cleaned and prepared the data in Python, SQL, and Excel
  • Studied Annual Income, Purchase History, Number of Purchases, Membership Tier, Loyalty Points, Satisfaction Score, Preferred Product Category, Purchase Channel, and Region
  • Identified behavior patterns across customer groups and turned them into recommendations
  • Built interactive dashboards that cut manual analysis effort by 35%

Insights and outcomes

  • High-value customers contributed a significant share of revenue
  • Higher membership tiers showed stronger engagement, and satisfied customers were generally more loyal
  • Product preferences differed across customer groups, and behavior varied by region and purchase channel
  • Recommended personalized campaigns, stronger loyalty programs, preference-based offers, and region-specific marketing
